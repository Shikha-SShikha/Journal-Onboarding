Welcome to Editorial Central

Before your first manuscript, a quick look at how things work

Editorial Central brings together evidence on scope, novelty, methodology and significance for each submission, to support the judgement you already bring.

The decisions stay with you
Suggestions are there to inform, and nothing moves a manuscript until you act.
Reasons alongside every signal
Each signal links to the passage or source it came from, so you can see how it was reached.
Room for uncertainty
When fit is unclear, you’ll see “Partial fit” with the reasons rather than a firm call. Missing evidence isn’t treated as a weakness in the paper.
Clear labels
The authors’ own words and AI-written text are marked, and you can choose how much of the AI interpretation you’d like to see.
VerbatimAI-written
Manuscripts kept private
Manuscript text isn’t sent to outside search services or compared with other publishers’ content.
Your disagreement counts
When you overrule a signal, it could be used to refine your journal’s settings.
